Sub: Constitution of Specialist Committees under Ayushman Bharat Pradhan
Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana- reg:

The competent authority has approved the constitution of 24 Specialist Committees
(Specialty-wise). Details of these committees is enclosed. These committees will advise
on matters pertaining to Standard Treatment Workflows (STWs) and Health Benefit
Packages (HBP) under the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB
PM_JAY). The Terms of Reference of the Specialist committees will be as follows:

¢ Recommend regarding how to remove aberrations /anomalies/
inconsistencies/ duplicates and unnecessary lateralisation, if any, existing in
the HBP.

¢ Recommend what to include and what to exclude in the HBP

e Advise on appropriate cost of health benefit packages based on the results of
costing study being undertaken

e Advise on mandatory documentation for admission and discharge under the
AB PM-JAY scheme for each of the packages

¢ Enable preparation of disease specific questionnaire for medical audit
purpose.

¢ Explore whether India can adopt WHO's International Classification of Health
Interventions (ICHI) or some other standard classification for coding its Health
benefit packages.

The recommendations of the specialist committees will be submitted to CEO, NHA.
These recommendations will be put up to the review committee.
