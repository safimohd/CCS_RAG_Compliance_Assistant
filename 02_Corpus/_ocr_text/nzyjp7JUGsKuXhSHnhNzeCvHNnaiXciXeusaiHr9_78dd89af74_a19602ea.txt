ADVISORY

Subject: Processing of application of Tempor Registration to the foreign medical

practitioners sponsored by any Institute/Hospital in the country- regarding

In this respect, kind attention is invited to the sub-section 13.1.2 of Section 13 under
chapter-IV (Fellowship/Clinical Research/Clinical Training/Observership etc.) of “Registration
of Medical Practitioners and Licence to Practice Medicine Regulation 2023. In this regard it may
be ensure by the sponsoring registered hospital/medical college/institute inviting foreign
national for fellowship programs the following provision have been made in the said regulation:

“i. A foreign citizen may be allowed to do Fellowship/Clinical Research/Clinical training /
Observership for enhancing their skill and competence through training or academic courses
conducted in any Institute in India, ii. For the purpose, the Institute may apply to the Ethics
and Medical Registration Board through the web portal along with the following details: a)
Name and registration certification of the foreign citizen (if applicable) b) Information
related to course content, duration, training facilities, teaching, and infrastructure facilities
(details should be available on the website of the Institute) c) Details of fee being charged by
the Institute......”

3. In view of the above in order to streamline the process of timely grant of temporary
Registration to Foreign Medical practitioners the following should be ensured:

The details of Fellowship programs being run by such registered hospital/medical
college/institute should be duly uploaded on their official website. along with information

. related to course content, duration, training facilities, teaching, faculty details (along with
active registration No) and infrastructure facilities etc. must be made available for online
verification of the same by EMRB (NMC) at the time of processing the application received
for grant of Temporary Registration.

4, Failure to comply with the above may cause delay in precessing the applications received
for temporary registration for fellowship programs. Ordinarily, temporary registration in respect
of the foreign medical practitioners which shall be provided for a maximum period of twelve (12)
months followed by a cooling off period of one year and shall be limited to the medical
college/institution/ hospital sponsoring the programme.
