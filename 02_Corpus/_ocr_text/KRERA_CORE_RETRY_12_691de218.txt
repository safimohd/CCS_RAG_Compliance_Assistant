“5, This Ministry has also issued advisory dated 13 May, 2020

months by invoking ‘Force Majeure’ clause under the provisions
of Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA).
Also, regulatory authorities, may on their own discretion,
consider to further extend the date of completion as per
registration for another period upto 3 months if situation needs
special consideration for whole or part of the State of invoking
‘Force Majeure’ in view of current pandemic. Such extension is to
be granted suo moto by revising the date of completion through
new letter of registration to be issued by RERA.”
