“Lok-Adalat”
“Advantages of the Lok-Adalat”

Lok - Adalat is organized to promote justices on the basis of equal
opportunity.

The Court/ Authority can direct both the parties to appear before Lok -
Adalat benches. The legal Service Authority will issue notification
constituting the Lok - Adalat benches consisting of two member i.e., one
Judicial Member and one Non- Judicial Member.

Quick Settlement of the cases.

No court fee is required to be paid by the parties.

Litigants can approach Lok - Adalat through their Advocates or in person.
Litigants will be guided suitably in process of settlement.

Amicable settlement of the cases which will resolve the dispute and restore
cordial relationship.

Litigants will have opportunity for settlement of pre- litigations cases before
the Lok - Adalat.

If the case is settled before the Lok - Adalat, Court fee already paid will be
refunded.

The settlement before the Lok - Adalat will be done with the consent of both
parties and there will be no force, compulsion to the parties to settle the
case.

If the case is settled, both the parties shall have to file Joint Memo/
Compromise Memo duly signed by them and the Member of Lok - Adalat
benches shall countersign it. Then an award will be passed.

The Order/ Award of Lok - Adalat is final and non- appealabie.

The five guiding principles of Lok - Adalat ie., is Accessible, Definitive,
Affordable, Equitable and timely Justice for all.
