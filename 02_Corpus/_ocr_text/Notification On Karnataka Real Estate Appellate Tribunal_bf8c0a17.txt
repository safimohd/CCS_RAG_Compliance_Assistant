NOTIFICATION
Whereas the Government of India have enacted the Real Estate
(Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (hereinafter referred to as the 'Act')
with effect from 26.03.2016;
Whereas the Government of Karnataka have notified the Karnataka
Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules,, 2017 (hereinafter referred
to as the 'Rules') in the Karnataka Gazette on 11.07 .2017;
Whereas the Government of Karnataka, vide Notification No. DOH
158 KHB 2017, dated 16.12.2017, had designated the "Karnataka Appellate
Tribunal" as the "Karnataka Interim Real Estate Appellate Tribunal" under
Section 43(4) of the said Act read with Rule 3l(9) of the said Rules;
Whereas the Government of Karnataka, vide Government Order No.
DOH 4 RERA 2017, dated 22.05.2018, have given approval for opening the
"Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Fund";
Whereas the Govemment of Karnataka, vide Notification No. DOH 24
RERA 2017, dated 08.03.2019, have appointed Justice Shri B. Sreenivase
Gowda, Former Judge of the High Court of Kamataka as the Chairperson of
the Karnataka Real Estate Tribunal; and the said Chairperson has assumed
charge on 13.03.2019;
Whereas the Government of Karnataka, vide Notification No. DOH 24
RERA 2017, dated 05.03.2019, have appointed Shri Dinesh K.P., as Judicial
Member of the Kamataka Real Estate Appellate Tribunal; and the said
Member has assumed charge on 09.05.2019;
Whereas the Government of Karnataka, vide Notification No. DO}l 24
RERA 2017, dated 05.03.2019, have appointed Shri Somashekar. P.S., as
Administrative Member of the Karnataka Real Estate Appellate Tribunal; and
the said Member has assumed charge on 08.03 .2019;
Now, in exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of the
Section 43 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act,2016, the
Government of Kamataka hereby establishes for the State of Karnataka, the
Tribunal to be known as "The Karnataka Real Estate Appellate Tribunal"
which shall have its headquarters at Bengaluru. "The Karnataka Real Estate
Appellate Tribunal" is deemed to have come into existence with effect from
13.03.2019, the day which the Chairperson of the said Authority has assumed
the charge. The said Tribunal shall function with effect from 03.01 .2020.
"The Karnataka Real Estate Appellate Tribunal" shall function at the
