Subject:- Advisory to All Hospitals Regarding Misleading Claims by Private Agencies for
Hospital Empanelment

It has come to the attention of the State Health Agency (SHA), Haryana through various sources
that certain private agencies are falsely claiming to assist hospitals in getting empanclled under the
Ayushman Bharat Haryana Health Protection Authority (AB-HHPA), often in exchange for fees or
other considerations.

In this regard, SHA Haryana issues the following advisory for all hospitals:

1. SHA Haryana does not authorize or endorse any private agency or consultant to
facilitate hospital empanelment under AB-HHPA.

i)

The hospital empanelment process is entirely online, paperless, and free of cost. No
charges or fees are required or accepted at any stage by SHA Haryana.

. Hospitals are advised to stay vigilant and not engage with any agency or individual
claiming to expedite or influence the empanelment process.

uo

4, Ifany hospital voluntarily enters into any agreement with such agencies, the full
responsibility will lie with the concerned hospital. SHA Haryana will not be a party to
any dispute, litigation, or financial loss arising out of such engagements.

5. It is reiterated that empanelment can only be done through the official process as
outlined on the AB-HHPA portal. No external facilitation is involved or entertained.
Hospitals are strongly advised to rely solely on official communications from SHA Haryana
and to report any such misleading claims to the concerned DNO immediately.
