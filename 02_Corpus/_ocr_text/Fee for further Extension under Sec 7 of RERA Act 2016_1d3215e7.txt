PREAMBLE: Section 7 of the Real Estate (Regulations and Development) Act, 2016 provids that on a complaint or Suo motu on the recommendation of competent Authority revocation of Registration granted under section 5 when the promoter makes default in doing any thing required by or under the Act. Many of the promoters are not completing the projects even after the extension of time. Section 7 (3) of the Act provides that the Auhtority instead of revoking the registration under sub-section 7 (i) permit it to remain in force subject to further terms and conditions as thinks fit to impose in the interest of the allottees. The conditions imposed shall be binding upon the promoter. The Authority had decided to levy fees for the cases which are extended under section 7 (i). Hence the following order. ORDER In the circumstances explained in the preamble, as per provision of section 34(e) and 85 (h) of Real Estale (Regulations and Development) Act; 20 J 6 for the projects not completed even after extension of the time, further extension is considered for allowing the promoter to make fresh application for registration by paying the necessclJY half of the fees as la.rd down in 3(3) of Rule, This order comes to effect from the date of issue of this order.
