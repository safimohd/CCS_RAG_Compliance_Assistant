both parties and there will be no force, compulsion to the parties to
settle the casc.

» If the case is settled, both the parties shall have to file Joint
Memo/Compromise Memo duly signed by them and the Member of
Lok - Adalat benches shall countersign it. Then an award will be
passed.

» The Order/ Award of Lok - Adalat is final and non- appealable.

» The five guiding principles of Lok Adalat i.e., is Accessible, Definitive,
Affordable, Equitable and timely Justice for all.
