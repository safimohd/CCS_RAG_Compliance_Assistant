Government of India has enacted the Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Act, 2016 and Government of Karnataka has notified the Karnataka Real Estate
(Regulation and Development) Rules 2017. Accordingly, the Karnataka Real
Estate Regulatory Authority (K-RERA) was established and a web portal was
created to enable the Real Estate Promoter/s to submit Annual Audit Report and
for other compliances.

As per section 4(2)(/)(D) of the RERA Act 2016, mandates the promoter/s to get
accounts audited within six months after the end of every financial year by a
Chartered Accountant in practice and shall produce a statement of accounts duly
certified and signed by Chartered Accountant and it shall be verified during the
audit the amount collected for particular project has been utilized for the project
and the withdrawal has been in compliance with the proportion to the percentage
of the completion of the project.

In exercise of the power conferred under section 25 and 37of the Real Estate
(Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory
Authority has notified the format of Annual Audit Report (Form-7).

Accordingly, Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority has enabled the facility
on its website for online filing of Annual Audit Report for the registered projects
and online model/ format for submission of Annual Audit Report (Form-7) was
published on K RERA web portal on 13-10-2022 to facilitate the promoter/s.

Further, as per Section 38(1) of the Real Estate (Regulatory and Development)
Act, 2016 the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority is vested with the
power to impose penalty or interest regarding any contravention of obligations
cast upon the promoter/s under provisions of the Act or the Rules and the
Regulations made thereunder.

In pursuant to the Circulars dated 20-10-2023 and 01-12-2023, the time for
submission of the Annual Audit Report for the Financial Year 2022-23 was
extended upto 31-12-2023.

In continuation of the said circulars, the Authority has issued notice through
offline/online mode to the promoters who have not submitted their Annual Audit
Report for the financial year 2022-23 and given sufficient opportunity to submit
their Annual Report for said period till date. Further, it is noticed by the
Authority that, some of the promoters have not yet submitted their Annual Audit
report for the financial year 2022-23 and not complied with the direction of the
Authority despite providing sufficient opportunity till date.

As such, the promoter/s who fails to submit the Annual Audit Report for the
financial year 2022-23 on or before 31-12-2023, such promoter/s shall be liable
to pay the penalty as required u/s 60 of the RERA Act.

Hence, it is decided by the Authority to levy the penalty for non-
submission/Delay in submission of Annual Audit Report for the financial year
2022-23. The penalty for delayed submission and non-submission of Annual
Audit Report shall be enforceable and all such promoter/s shall file the Annual
Audit Report for the financial year 2022-23 and pay the penalty through RERA e-
Payment.The provision for penalty payment will be made available under the
option of Annual Audit Report submission on the KRERA portal.
