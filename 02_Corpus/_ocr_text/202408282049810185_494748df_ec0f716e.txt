Subject: - Withholding the process of empanelment of new hospitals under

Ayushman Bharat due to implementation of Model Code of Conduct in the State of
Haryana

With reference to the subject cited above, it is bring under your kind notice
that the model code of conduct has been implemented in the state of Haryana vide dated
16/08/2024. Due to implementation of model code of conduct, the permission of Election
Commission is required to conduct State Empanelment Committee (SEC) meeting and to
empanel the new hospitals under Ayushman Bharat scheme.

In view of above, it is to inform you that the process of hospital
empanelment of new hospitals has been kept on hold due to implementation of model code
of conduct in the State of Haryana. You are hereby requested to inform all the concerned
hospitals of your district in this regard. The procedure for empanelment of new hospitals
will start as soon as the permission is granted/ clarification is received from the Election
Commission.

This is for your information and necessary action please.
