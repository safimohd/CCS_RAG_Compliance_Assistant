Sub: Guidelines for Change of Project Name - Reg.

seek

Whereas, under Section 37 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act,
2016 ("said Act") the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority is vested with the
power to issue directions from time to time to the promoters, allottees or real estate
agents' as it considers necessary from time to time.

Whereas, the Chairperson, Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority is also

vested with the powers of general superintendence and directions in the conduct of
affairs of the Authority under section 25 of the said Act.

Whereas, the Authority has been receiving various representations for providing an
opportunity to change the Project Name provided to the Authority at the time of making
an application.

Whereas, under powers granted to the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority
and its Chairman under the aforesaid provisions of the said Act and since the Karnataka
Real Estate Regulatory Authority considers that it is necessary in the interest of the
promoters including landowners and the allottees, the present Guidelines is issued
exercising powers under the aforesaid provisions of the said Act.

Therefore, to provide necessary guideline to promoters of Real Estate Projects,
regarding the appropriate nature of compliance for change of project name, the authority
hereby annexed the format as provided in the Authority web portal for filing Application for
Change of Project Name and the Checklist No.1 and 2 to submit and to this Guidelines
for compliance to be ensured while seeking for change in project name.

All promoters of real estate projects shall be adhered to it full, so as to avoid non-
compliance which may lead to Regulatory action under the Act and Rules.
