OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Subject: Regarding increase in the maximum age of children for whom the
Aadhaar authentication by parents may be used for availing the services
under PM-JAY

As per current policy of Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, any child upto the
age of 1 year can avail treatment at empanelled hospitals under the scheme
using their parents’ Ayushman card. Parents are also required to undergo
Aadhaar based authentication for the purposes of e-KYC at the time of seeking
services under AB PM-JAY for their children of age less than 5 years.
