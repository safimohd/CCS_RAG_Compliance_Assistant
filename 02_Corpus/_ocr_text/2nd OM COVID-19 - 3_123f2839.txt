OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Sub: Testing and treatment for COVID-19 under Ayushman Bharat
Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojna.

With reference to the OM of even number dated 4¢h April 2020, it is further
clarified that.
1. For testing, payment shall be made in the following circumstances:

a. Public hospitals having tie up with private labs.
b. Private hospitals doing testing

Note: No payment shall be made to public hospitals utilising their own or other
public testing facility

2. For treatment, payment shall be made to both public and private hospitals at
the rate decided by the respective state government.

It may also be noted that the payment for PM-JAY beneficiaries shall be made
by the National Health Authority as per the current mechanism of sharing the

financial burden. ms
