Circular on Registration of Real Estate Agents

Government of India has enacted the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016
and Government of Karnataka has notified the Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Rules,2017. Accordingly, the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) was established,
and a web portal was created to enable the real estate agents to register themselves online by

submittingapplication for grant of registration.

Section 9 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA Act 2016) read with
Rule 9 of Karnataka RERA Rules 2017 that every real estate agent shall obtain the registration from
the authority before facilitate the sale or purchase of or act on behalf of any person to facilitate the
sale or purchase of any plot, apartment or building, as the case may be, in a real estate project or part

of it, being the part of the real estate project registered under section 3, being sold by the promoter.

It has come to the notice of this Authority that many agents are facilitating the real estate transaction
in contravention to Section 9 of the RERA Act 2016.

In exercise of the power conferred under section 25 and 37 of the Real Estate (Regulation and
Development) Act, 2016 and as the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority having considered it

necessary to issue this circular calling upon all such agents to register within 30 March 2022.

In case of the non-compliance, the same shall amount to an offence under Section 62 of RERA Act
2016 and, the Authority shall take appropriate action as against such agent in accordance with the
Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Rules, 2017 by levying penalty which may cumulatively extend up to five percent of the cost of plot,
apartment or buildings, as the case may be, of the real estate project, for which the sale or purchase

has been facilitated as determined by the Authority.

(Approved by the Hon’ble Authority)
