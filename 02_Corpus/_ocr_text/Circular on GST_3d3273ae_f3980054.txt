2017, there is a provision to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) on both the Goods

purchasers in many cases. If such is the case, the purchasers who do not get
these benefits may complain against the builders with full details to State
Screening Committee Commissioner of State Tax, or central GST NAA
(National Anti-profiteering Authority, GST) Complaints can also be lodged
