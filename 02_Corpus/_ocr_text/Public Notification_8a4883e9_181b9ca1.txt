PUBLIC NOTICE

This is inform to general public that the Real Estate (Regulation and
Development) Act 2016 has come into effect as on 1/5/2017 and with the
notification of the Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules
2017 On 11/07/2017,the Real Estate Regulatory Authority started functioning
and now the full time Real Estate Regulatory Authority is in place and
discharging its functions.

All the promoter/ Developer/ Builder are hereby informed that they shall not
advertise, market, book, sell or offer for sale or invite persons to purchase or
transfer of any plot, apartment or building in a real estate project having more
than 8 apartments/units or sites formed in the land for the project being more
than 500 square meters, without obtaining prior registration from Karnataka
Real Estate Regulatory Authority as required under section 4 of the Real Estate
Regulation and Development) Act 2016 and rule 3 of The Karnataka Real Estate
(Regulation and Development) Rules 2017, failing which such Promoter's will
become liable to be penalized as per Section 59 of the Act.

All Buyers are hereby informed that they should verify whether the project is
registered with KA-RERA by visiting the website www.rera.karnataka.gov.in.
Buyers are also informed that they can complain to the Authority for not
delivering the project as agreed by the builder/developer.

Before buying any apartment/plot/villa etc., in any real estate project and the
buyers are also cautioned not to purchase properties which are not approved
by the competent authorities/local bodies and not registered with KA-RERA
Estate Regulatory Authority.

Any persons coming across any such said violations may Unfit fe authority

by reporting such violations through the website www.rerdkarnataka,gov.in or
in writing. \ 4
