Sub: Constitution of Review Committee under Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan
Arogya Yojana- reg:

The competent authority has approved the constitution of a Review Committee. Details
of this committee is enclosed. This committee will review the matters put forward by the
Specialist committees pertaining to Standard Treatment Workflows (STWs) and Health
Benefit Packages (HBP) under the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana
(AB PM_JAY). The Terms of Reference of the Review committee will be as follows:

e Review the recommendations of the Specialist committees regarding- correction
of aberrations, anomalies, duplicates, inclusions exclusions, appropriate package
costing, mandatory documentation for admission and discharge, disease specific
questionnaire for medical audit, etc. in the HBP, and through the CEO NHA bring
the final recommendations to the Governing Board for approval.

e Review recommendations in light of the available resources, overall policy
direction, mandate of the program and make necessary modifications to align the
recommendations with the objectives of the AB PM-JAY.

Encl.: As above. -
