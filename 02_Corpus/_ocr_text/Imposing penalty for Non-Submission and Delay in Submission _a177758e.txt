As per Section 11 (1) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016
t/w Rule 15(1)(D) of the Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Rules, 2017, it is the duty of the promoter to perform certain functions/
obligations as required under the Act or the Rules and the Regulations made
thereunder. One of the mandatory requirements is to submit the respective
quarterly updates through K-RERA web portal.

Accordingly, the promoter shall submit the quarterly updates of their real estate
project/s through K-RERA web portal within Fifteen days from the expiry of
each Quarter i.e., on or before 15" of July for the 1 Quarter (Q1), on or before
15" of October for the 2" Quarter (Q2), on or before 15" of January for 3“!
Quarter (Q3) and on or before 15" of April for 4"" Quarter (Q4).

Under Section 35 and Section 37 of the Real Estate (Regulatory and
Development) Act, 2016, the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority is
vested with the power to issue directions for the purpose of discharging its
functions under the provisions of the Act or the Rules and the Regulations made
thereunder.

Accordingly, Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority has enabled the facility
on its website for online filing of the respective Quarterly Updates for the
registered projects since Quarter-2 of 2018-19.

Further, as per Section 38(1) of the Real Estate (Regulatory and Development)
Act, 2016, the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority is vested with the
power to impose penalty or interest in regards to any contravention of obligations

The authority hereby brought to the attention of the promoters that as per the
Power vested u/s Section 38(1), if any promoters contravenes any other
provisions of the Act, other than that provided under Section 3 or Section 4, or

project as determined by the Authority by invoking Section 61 of the RERA Act.

As per the observation made by this Authority, few of the promoters have
defaulted in filing their respective quarterly reports on the website of the
Authority, within the prescribed time in accordance with section 11(1) of the Act
t/w Rule 15(1)(D) of Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development)
Rules, 2017.

Hence, it is decided by the Authority to levy penalty of Rs.25,000/- (Twenty Five
Thousand Only) for every Quarterly report for non-submission/delay in
submission of respective quarterly reports for the Financial Year 2025-26 in
compliance with the provisions of the Act or the Rules and the Regulations made
thereunder. The penalty for delayed submission/non-submission of respective
quarterly report/s shall be enforceable and all such promoters shall file/submit the
respective quarterly updates for the Financial Year 2025-26 and to pay the
penalty through RERA e-Payment. The provision for penalty payment will be
made available under K-RERA web portal through promoter’s respective Project
Login for Quarterly Report submission.

A final opportunity is hereby extended up to 20-02-2026 to the promoters of real
estate projects to submit the quarterly reports i.e., for Quarter-1 of 2025-26,
Quarter-2 of 2025-26 and Quarter-3 of 2025-26, without payment of penalty. In
the event of failure on the part of the promoter/s to submit the quarterly reports
for the above said three quarters on or before 20-02-2026, for each delayed
quarterly report, the promoter shall be liable to pay a penalty of Rs.25,000/-
